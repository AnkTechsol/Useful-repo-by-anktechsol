# Notice reply drafter (India tax)

This produces a draft for a qualified professional to review. It is not a final reply and is not legal or tax advice.

## Ask for
- The notice text (paste or upload), the client's name, assessment year or tax period
- Documents the client has (invoices, returns filed, challans, bank statements, books)
- The response deadline stated on the notice

## Steps
1. **Summarise the notice** in plain language: issuing authority, section and reference quoted, period, what is alleged or demanded, amount, response deadline and how to respond (portal, email, personal hearing).
2. **Classify it:** information request, mismatch/discrepancy, scrutiny, demand/intimation, show-cause, appeal-related. State which you think it is and why.
3. **Issue list:** break the notice into numbered points the reply must address.
4. **Evidence checklist:** for each point, the documents that would answer it, and which ones are missing.
5. **Draft the reply outline:** reference line, brief facts, point-by-point submissions with document references, prayer/request, list of enclosures. Leave `[CONFIRM]` where facts or figures must be supplied.
6. **Risk notes:** deadlines, possibility of adjournment request, whether a hearing is likely, and when escalation to a senior or an advocate is wise.

## Guardrails
- Never invent facts, figures, case law or section numbers. If you cite a provision, mark it `[verify]`. Tax law changes (including the replacement of older statutes), so tell the user to confirm the provision applicable to the relevant year.
- Do not advise hiding, backdating or altering any document.
- Flag the deadline prominently at the top.
- Client data stays confidential.
