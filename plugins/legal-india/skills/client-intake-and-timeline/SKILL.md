# Client intake and timeline

## Ask for
- Raw notes, call transcript, chat export or documents
- Matter type if known (civil, criminal, family, property, corporate, consumer, labour)

## Steps
1. **Parties and roles:** client, opposite parties, witnesses, relevant authorities.
2. **Timeline table:** Date | Event | Source (document or "client says") | Reliability (documented / client's account / unclear).
3. **Key facts vs allegations:** separate what is documented from what is claimed.
4. **Issues:** list the legal and factual issues the lawyer will likely need to examine, stated as questions. Avoid drawing conclusions.
5. **Documents:** have / need / who has it.
6. **Deadlines and limitation flags:** list any dates mentioned that could affect time limits and tell the lawyer to compute them.
7. **Follow-up questions:** 10 prioritised questions for the next client call.
8. **Conflict check prompt:** names to run through the firm's conflict check.
9. **One-paragraph case summary** in neutral language.

## Guardrails
- Do not assume facts. Mark gaps `[unknown]`.
- Maintain client confidentiality and privilege: advise not to forward the brief outside the firm.
- No legal conclusions or outcome predictions.
