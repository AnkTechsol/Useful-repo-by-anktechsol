# NDA drafter (India)

## Ask for
- Type: mutual or one-way (who discloses)
- Party names, entity types, addresses, and purpose of disclosure
- Term of the agreement and how long confidentiality lasts after it ends
- What counts as confidential (technical, business, personal data) and standard exclusions
- Governing law and place of dispute resolution (courts or arbitration; seat and language)
- Whether employees or contractors of the parties will receive the information

## Steps
1. Produce the NDA with these clauses: parties, definitions, purpose, obligations, permitted disclosures, exclusions (public domain, already known, independently developed, lawfully received), compelled disclosure, return or destruction, term, remedies (injunctive relief is typically sought), no licence, no obligation to proceed, governing law, dispute resolution, notices, entire agreement, counterparts and e-signature note, signature block.
2. After the draft, add a **"What each clause means"** section in plain language.
3. Add an **execution checklist:** stamp paper or e-stamping requirement under the relevant state's rules (confirm current rules), signing authority, witnesses if desired.
4. If the user supplies another party's NDA, instead produce a comparison and risk table: unusual term length, one-sided definitions, overbroad non-compete or non-solicit, missing exclusions, onerous penalties.

## Guardrails
- Penalty or liquidated damages clauses must be reasonable; flag them for lawyer review.
- Do not insert restrictive non-compete terms by default.
- Mark placeholders `[ ]`. Do not invent party details.
- This is a template-level draft. Recommend lawyer review before signing.
